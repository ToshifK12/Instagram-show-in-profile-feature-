Show in profile

A feature concept for Reels. Tap the blue button and the profile opens at the exact reel you were watching, so Part 2 is right next to it.

  1. Tap Show in profile on the reel.
  2. The grid scrolls to the reel you were watching (blue ring).
  3. Tap the Part 2 tile next to it.
